# Identity board generation

The consolidated board, [identity-board.png](identity-board.png), dates from 10 November 2026. It was produced from a reference image of the aligned mark and the prompt below. Only the final board is kept in this folder.

## Prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Produce a polished consolidated brand identity board, landscape 3:2, for the Android photo-transfer app Camera Connect. Attached image is a reference for the agreed identity. Use ONLY its leftmost Aligned logo: two concentric ivory toothed C-shaped camera dial rings, both openings aligned right, generous dark gap between rings, orange index mark on top of OUTER ring. Preserve this design closely, no alternate logo concepts. Public name must be exactly "Camera Connect" everywhere, title case with a space, never lowercase or hyphenated.
Board on warm ivory with elegant editorial grid, generous whitespace, crisp accurate concise text. Top heading "Camera Connect", subtitle "Visual identity / Precision", small footer "Identity direction · November 2026". Left upper large hero charcoal rounded square app icon containing the aligned double C dial. Next to it horizontal logo plus bold grotesk sans wordmark Camera Connect, and compact monochrome charcoal-on-ivory logo specimen. Label "Aligned double C" and short line "Inspired by knurled camera dials". Shapes have short shallow grip teeth, not long gear teeth; no gradients or bevels. Orange index only on outer ring. Clean flat ivory logo shapes.
Middle left four exact palette swatches and legible labels: "Charcoal #17191C", "Graphite #2B2E33", "Ivory #F3F1EB", "Orange #F36B45". Typography specimen below: "Camera Connect" in bold modern grotesk sans and "Clear. Precise. Photographic." in regular clean sans, label "Typography direction". Do not claim a specific font family.
Lower left material samples: subtle fine monochromatic photographic grain over charcoal next to macro of unbranded black knurled camera dial. Label "Fine grain / Matte surfaces". Short line "Crisp type. Untouched photos."
Right third ONE large flat front-facing Android home screen concept on charcoal fine-grain surface, header small aligned double-C logo and "Camera Connect", heading "Your camera.", device card "Sony A7R IV" and "Connected", orange button with DARK CHARCOAL text "Open gallery", section "Latest photos", two vivid natural unfiltered landscape photo thumbnails. No fake camera photo needed in device card; use clean simple device details. Text crisp, photo thumbnails entirely free of grain, grain only on background and device card. Small label below screen "Illustrative interface".
Finish: restrained premium precision photographic instrument identity, fine subtle static grain, no heavy distress, no alternative colors, no extra logos, no fake official Sony endorsement, no other concept variants. Readable typo-free text, logo consistent across all specimens. This is a visual direction board, not final vector artwork.
```
